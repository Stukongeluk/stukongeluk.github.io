More about myself

Hello there! I'm Jimmy Nguyen, a Senior DevOps Engineer & Full-Stack Developer based in Leiden, Netherlands. My journey into computer science began in 2014 at Hogeschool Leiden, diving deep into core software craftsmanship, algorithms, and distributed architectures.

In 2016, I kickstarted my professional career as a Junior Developer, marking the beginning of what has now become 10 years of foundational software craftsmanship. Over the years, I've built, scaled, and debugged complex systems across Public Sector & Taxation (Belastingdienst), Energy (Essent), Climate (KNMI), Media (DPG Media), Mobility (Qbuzz), and high-growth SaaS (HeadFirst Group, Flottando, Capydrive). I combine deep architectural fundamentals with Forward Deploy execution—taking complete ownership from infrastructure to client delivery.

Whether architecting zero-downtime Auth0 identity migrations, building Golang DevEx CLI tools, or deploying Kubernetes and serverless platforms, I pragmatically leverage modern AI tooling (Claude, MCP) as an efficiency amplifier to accelerate developer velocity and ship dependable systems.
